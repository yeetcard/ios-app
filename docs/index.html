Yeetcard

Scan, store, and pull up your loyalty cards — private and offline.

Yeetcard keeps all your loyalty, membership, and rewards cards in one place on your device, so you can leave the plastic at home. Scan a barcode once, then pull it up full-screen at checkout with the brightness boosted so it always scans.

  • Scan or type any barcode or QR code.
  • Full-screen display with automatic brightness boost.
  • Private by design — everything stays on your device, no account, no tracking.
  • Locked behind Face ID / Touch ID.
