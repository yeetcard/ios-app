Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Yeetcard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All cards you add — including images, barcode data, names, and notes — are stored only on your device.

  • We do not operate servers that receive your data. The app makes no network connections in normal use.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • Camera access is used solely, on your device, to scan the barcodes and QR codes on your own cards.
  • Microphone access is used solely, on your device, to detect a beep that auto-advances cards in group view. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Photos access is used solely to import a card image you choose.
  • Your cards are protected on-device behind Face ID / Touch ID.

Because your data never leaves your device, deleting the app permanently and completely removes all of it. We have no way to access or recover it.
